---
title: Code of conduct
description: How we treat each other.
---

We want CROFF to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and kind.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who repeatedly do so. A contact address for reports will be published with the public repository.
